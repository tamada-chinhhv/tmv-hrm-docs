# Leave & Time Off Requests

**When to use:** Request time off for vacation, unpaid leave, late arrivals, early departures, remote work, family events, or other absences.

## Creating a Leave Request

### Step-by-step

1. Go to **Time & Attendance** → **Leave Requests**
2. Click **Create Request**
3. Choose the **type of leave** (e.g., Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Unpaid Leave)
4. Set your **dates and times**
5. Add a **reason** (optional but helpful)
6. Pick your **approver** (usually your manager)
7. Submit and wait for approval

### Types of Leave

- **Annual Leave** - Paid vacation days
- **Sick Leave** - Time off due to illness
- **Unpaid Leave** - Days not paid by the company
- **Late Arrival** - Coming in late to work
- **Early Departure** - Leaving work early
- **Remote Work** - Working from outside the office
- **Other** - As defined by your company policy

## Understanding Your Leave Balance

### Leave Remaining Display

Your available leave time shows at the top of the leave request form. It displays in one of two ways:

**By Days:** Example shows `5.5 days remaining`
- One full work day = 1 day

**By Minutes:** Example shows `440 minutes remaining`
- Full work day = 480 minutes (8 hours)
- Half day = 240 minutes (4 hours)
- You can switch between these views using the dropdown button

### Example Calculations

- If you have **8 hours (480 minutes)** of leave left and take **4 hours (240 minutes)** off, you'll have **4 hours (240 minutes)** remaining
- If you have **10 days** and take 1 day off, you'll have **9 days** remaining

## Late Arrival & Early Departure

### What is a "Late Arrival" request?

If you arrive later than your scheduled start time, you can request a **Late Arrival** to explain those minutes.

**Example:**
- Your work starts at 8:00 AM
- You arrive at 8:30 AM
- You submit a **Late Arrival** request for **30 minutes**

### What is an "Early Departure" request?

If you leave before your scheduled end time, you can request an **Early Departure** for those minutes.

**Example:**
- Your work ends at 5:00 PM
- You leave at 4:30 PM
- You submit an **Early Departure** request for **30 minutes**

---

## Unpaid Leave Option for Late/Early

### When Late/Early Becomes "Unpaid"

If you don't have enough leave balance to cover your late arrival or early departure, the system shows you an option:

**You can choose:**
1. **Cancel the request** - Don't submit it
2. **Charge as Unpaid** - Those minutes will be deducted without using your paid leave balance

### Unpaid Badge

When a late arrival or early departure is marked as **unpaid**, a red badge appears showing:

**Badge label:** `Unpaid`

This means those minutes **will not be paid** and won't count against your leave balance. This is useful when:
- You've already used all your leave time
- You had a brief personal emergency
- Your company policy allows unpaid time off

### Important Note

Only **late arrivals and early departures** can be marked as unpaid. Full-day leave requests cannot.

## Advance Leave Requests (Future Requests)

### What is an Advance Leave Request?

An **advance leave request** lets you request time off for a **future date** without specifying exact dates yet. This is useful when:
- You know you'll need a few days off but haven't scheduled the exact dates
- You want to book leave in advance for a project or event
- You need to commit time off without planning the exact dates yet

### How to Create an Advance Request

1. Go to **Time & Attendance** → **Leave Requests**
2. Click **Create Request**
3. Choose your **leave type**
4. Instead of selecting dates, choose **Advance Leave** option (if available)
5. Enter **how much time** you need:
   - In **Days**: `5 days`
   - In **Hours**: `40 hours`
   - In **Minutes**: `2400 minutes`
6. Select your **approver**
7. Add a **reason** (optional)
8. Submit for approval

### Example

You want to request 3 days off for a personal project next month but haven't picked the exact dates yet.

1. Create an **Advance Leave Request** for **3 days**
2. Get it approved
3. Later, when you know the exact dates, you submit another request using those approved advance days

## Tracking Your Requests

### View Request Status

In the **Leave Requests** list, you can see:
- **Status**: Waiting for approval / Approved / Rejected
- **Type**: The leave type
- **Dates**: When the leave is scheduled
- **Reason**: Why you requested it (if provided)

### Actions While Pending

If your request is **still waiting for approval**, you can usually:
- **Edit** the details
- **Delete** (cancel) the entire request

Once approved, you cannot edit or delete it.

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't submit my request"

**Check these things:**
- Did you select a **leave type**? (Required)
- Did you pick your **approver**? (Required)
- For regular leave: Did you choose **start and end dates**? (Required)
- Do you have **enough leave balance**? (If not, you can mark as unpaid for late/early only)

### "It says I don't have enough leave balance"

**You have options:**
1. **Request less time** - Adjust your dates or minutes
2. **Use unpaid leave** - If it's a late arrival or early departure, you can mark it as unpaid
3. **Wait for more leave** - Annual leave may reset on a specific date each year
4. **Contact your HR team** - They can discuss other options with you

### "My late arrival/early departure was rejected"

**Why this happens:**
- Your approver may not have noticed it
- Your request might not follow company policy
- You may have exceeded limits on unpaid time off

**What to do:**
- Contact your **manager or HR team** to discuss
- Ask what specific reason it was rejected
- Resubmit with additional explanation if needed
