# Clock In & Clock Out (Attendance Tracking)

**When to use:** Every day when you arrive at work and when you leave.

## How to Clock In (Arrival)

### Step-by-step

1. Go to **Time & Attendance** → **Clock In/Out**
2. Click **Clock In** when you arrive at work
3. **On web:** Allow the browser to access your location (GPS)
4. **On mobile:** Make sure you're connected to the company WiFi before clocking in
5. The system records your arrival time

### Result

You'll see a message showing your **arrival time recorded**. It appears in your daily attendance record.

---

## How to Clock Out (Departure)

### Step-by-step

1. Go to **Time & Attendance** → **Clock In/Out**
2. Click **Clock Out** when you leave work
3. **On web:** Allow the browser to access your location (GPS)
4. **On mobile:** Make sure you're still on company WiFi
5. The system records your departure time

### Result

The system now knows when you **left the office**. This completes your attendance for that day.

---

## Understanding Attendance Status

Each day at work shows a **status** that tells you what kind of day it was. Here are the common ones:

### Status Types

**Normal Work Days:**
- **WORK** - You clocked in and out normally
- **LATE_EARLY** - You arrived late or left early (with a request submitted)

**Days Off:**
- **WEEKEND** - Saturday or Sunday (not a work day)
- **HOLIDAY** - Company holiday or public holiday
- **LEAVE** - You had approved time off

**Missing Clock:**
- **ABSENT** - You didn't clock in or out
- **FORGOT_CLOCK_IN** - System detected you didn't clock in but you were working
- **NOT_EMPLOYED** - This status appears as a **dash (-)** - This means the system doesn't have your employment information for that date (you may not have been employed yet, or employment ended)

**Special Status:**
- **FUTURE** - The date is in the future (shows as a dash **-**)

---

## What Does the "NOT_EMPLOYED" Status Mean?

### When You See a Dash (-)

If a date in your calendar shows a **dash (-)** instead of a status:

**It means:** The system doesn't recognize you as an active employee for that date

**This can happen:**
- **Before your start date** - You weren't employed yet
- **After your employment ended** - You left the company
- **During a leave of absence** - You're temporarily not active
- **Data issue** - Missing employment information in the system

### What You Should Do

If you see **dashes (-) for dates when you WERE working:**

1. **Check your employment dates** - Ask HR if your start/end dates are correct
2. **Contact your HR team** - Report that your dates seem wrong
3. **Don't worry about clocking in** - If the system shows NOT_EMPLOYED, you likely can't clock in anyway

## Troubleshooting

### "I get an 'outside office zone' error"

**This means:** The GPS shows you're not near the office

**How to fix it:**
- Move to the office location (GPS location check)
- Make sure you're close to the office building
- Check that your phone's location is turned on and accurate

### "I can't clock in on mobile"

**Check:**
- Are you connected to **company WiFi**? (Some companies use WiFi instead of GPS)
- Is your phone's **location turned on**?
- Is the **app allowed to access your location**? (Check phone settings)

**If still not working:**
- Try clocking in on a **computer/web** version instead
- Ask your IT team to check if your phone is registered

### "I already have an approved 'Remote Work' request today"

**Good news:** If you have approved remote work for that day, you can:
- Clock in and out **without needing GPS or WiFi**
- Be anywhere in the world
- Just use the app normally

### "I forgot to clock in (or out)"

**What happens:**
- Your manager or HR can see you forgot
- System may mark it as **FORGOT_CLOCK_IN**
- You can request a correction

**How to fix it:**
1. Ask your **manager or HR team** to help
2. Or submit a **leave request** with type **"Attendance Correction"**
3. Provide the **time you actually arrived/left**
4. They'll review and update your record

### "I clocked in/out but it's not showing"

**Wait a moment** - The system sometimes takes a few seconds to update

**If it still doesn't show after 1 minute:**
- Refresh the page
- Try logging out and back in
- Contact IT if the problem continues

## Daily Attendance Calendar View

### What You See Each Month

**On the dashboard**, you can see a calendar showing:
- Your **clock in and out times**
- Your **attendance status** for each day (WORK, LEAVE, WEEKEND, etc.)
- **Special symbols** for different types of days

### Color Codes

Different statuses may have different colors to help you quickly understand your attendance:
- **Green** - Normal work day
- **Blue** - Day off / Weekend
- **Yellow** - Partial day / Late/Early
- **Red** - Absent or issue

## Tips for Smooth Attendance Tracking

✓ **Clock in immediately when you arrive** - Don't wait  
✓ **Clock out before you leave** - Don't forget at the end of the day  
✓ **Enable location permissions** - Give the app access to GPS  
✓ **Connect to company WiFi** on mobile - Check with IT if needed  
✓ **Report problems early** - Don't wait weeks to fix clocking issues  
✓ **Keep your start date updated** - Make sure HR has your correct employment dates

## Working Remotely

### If You Have Approved Remote Work

**You can:**
- Clock in and out from anywhere
- Don't need GPS or WiFi
- Just use the app normally

### If You DON'T Have Approved Remote Work

**You should:**
- Be at the office to clock in/out
- Use the office GPS/WiFi
- Request remote work if you need to work from home
