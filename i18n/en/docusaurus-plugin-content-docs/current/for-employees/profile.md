# Your Profile & Avatar

**When to use:** View or update your personal information, upload a profile picture, or manage your account settings.

---

## Accessing Your Profile

### Step-by-step

1. Click your **name or avatar** in the top right corner of the screen
2. Select **Account** or **My Account**
3. You'll see your profile information

---

## Understanding Your Avatar (Profile Picture)

### What is an Avatar?

An **avatar** is your **profile picture** that shows next to your name throughout the system. Other people see it when:
- They look at your name in a document or request
- They see your comment or message
- They view a list of employees

### How Your Avatar Displays

Your avatar appears in **three different ways**, depending on what you have:

**Option 1: If you uploaded a photo**
- Your personal **photo** displays in a circle
- Clear and recognizable to others
- Great for connecting faces to names

**Option 2: If you don't have a photo (Default: Initials)**
- **Your initials** display instead
- Example: If your name is "John Smith", it shows **JS**
- Example: If your name is "Linh Ngô", it shows **LN**
- Each letter is one initial from your name
- Displays in a colored circle (color is randomly assigned)
- Still clear and professional-looking

**Option 3: If you have a partial name**
- The system shows **as many initials as your name has**
- Example: One-word name "Madonna" shows **M**
- Example: Three-word name "Mary Jane Watson" shows **MJW**

## Uploading a Profile Photo

### How to Upload

1. Go to **Account** → **Profile** or **My Account**
2. Click on your current **avatar or photo**
3. Select **Upload Photo** or **Change Avatar**
4. Choose a **photo from your device**
5. The system may ask you to **crop it** to fit the circle
6. Click **Save** or **Confirm**

### Photo Requirements

- **Format:** JPG, PNG, or similar image file
- **Size:** Keep under 5 MB (very reasonable for most phone photos)
- **Content:** Professional photo recommended (headshot or professional image)
- **Visibility:** Your photo will be seen by colleagues and managers

### Cropping Your Photo

If your photo doesn't fit perfectly in the circle:

1. The system shows you a **crop preview**
2. You can **drag and zoom** to adjust what shows in the circle
3. Try to **center your face** or the main part of the image
4. Click **Done** when satisfied

### Removing Your Photo

If you want to go back to **initials only**:

1. Go to your **Profile**
2. Find the **"Remove Photo"** or **"Delete Avatar"** option
3. Confirm the action
4. Your initials will display again

## Viewing & Editing Your Information

### What Information Displays

Your profile shows:
- **Full Name** - Your official name as registered
- **Email** - Your work email address
- **Phone** - Your phone number (optional)
- **Department** - Which team/department you're in
- **Job Title** - Your position
- **Avatar** - Your photo or initials

### What You Can Edit

Usually you can change:
- **Email** - Update if it changes
- **Phone Number** - Add or change your phone
- **Photo** - Upload a profile picture

### What You CANNOT Edit (Fixed by HR)

These are set by your company and HR team:
- **Full Name** - HR must approve name changes
- **Employee ID** - Your unique company ID
- **Department** - Set by management
- **Job Title** - Set by management
- **Start Date** - Your employment start date

**If you need to change any of these, ask your HR team.**

## Privacy & Visibility

### Who Sees Your Information?

**Your Profile is Visible To:**
- ✓ All employees in your company
- ✓ Your managers and HR team
- ✓ Anyone in the leave/attendance approval chain

**Your Photo Will Show:**
- ✓ Next to your name in requests and comments
- ✓ On your profile page
- ✓ In any system-wide search or directory

### Protecting Your Privacy

**You control:**
- Whether to upload a **real photo** (or use initials only)
- Your **phone number** (can be optional)
- **Email address** visibility (usually shown to HR)

**You cannot control:**
- **Your name** being visible to colleagues (it's in the system)
- **Your avatar** being shown next to your requests
- **Your department/title** visibility (work information)

## Troubleshooting

### "My avatar doesn't look right"

**Possible causes:**
- **Initials are wrong** - Check your full name spelling
- **Photo is cropped strangely** - Try re-uploading and cropping again
- **Initials showing instead of photo** - Your photo upload may have failed

**How to fix:**
1. Delete your current photo
2. Upload a new one
3. Make sure to save/confirm the upload
4. Refresh the page to see the change

### "I want to change my name but can't edit it"

**You cannot change your own name** - It's protected data

**What to do:**
1. Ask your **HR team**
2. Explain why you need the change (marriage, preference, legal change, etc.)
3. They'll update it in the system
4. The change will appear after they update it

### "My photo won't upload"

**Check:**
- Is the file a **valid image** (JPG, PNG)?
- Is it **smaller than 5 MB**?
- Did you click **Save** after uploading?
- Try a different image file

**If still not working:**
- Use a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- Clear your browser cache and try again
- Contact IT for help

### "I see someone else's initials instead of their photo"

**This is normal** - They either:
- Haven't uploaded a photo yet (using initials default)
- Chose to remove their photo
- Had a technical issue with their upload

**There's nothing wrong** - Initials work just fine as avatars.
