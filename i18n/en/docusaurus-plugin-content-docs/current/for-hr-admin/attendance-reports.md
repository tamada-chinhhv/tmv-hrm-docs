# Department Attendance Reports

## When to use

Use this feature to see a quick overview of attendance across your departments. Get a complete picture of who's present, who's absent, and who's on leave — all organized by department.

## What is the Department Attendance Report?

The Department Attendance Report shows you at a glance:

- **Total employees** in each department
- **Present today** — how many are working
- **On leave** — who approved leave (vacation, sick, etc.)
- **Late arrivals** — who checked in after the shift start time
- **Absent** — who didn't check in and didn't submit leave
- **Attendance rate** — percentage of employees who were present or on approved leave

Think of it as a "roll call" for each department, updated in real-time.

## Where to find the report

**Menu path:**

1. Go to **Attendance & Time** → **Attendance Tracking** (Chấm công & Thời gian → Theo dõi chấm công)
2. On the dashboard, you'll see **Attendance by Department** section
3. Each department shows a summary card with today's attendance

## Understanding the report

### Attendance status colors

The report uses colors to make information easy to scan:

- **Green** — Present (checked in on time)
- **Yellow/Orange** — Late (checked in after shift start)
- **Blue** — On approved leave
- **Red** — Absent (no check-in and no approved leave)
- **Gray** — Not assigned to a shift (e.g., new employee, future hire)

### Department card breakdown

Each department card shows:

| Info | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Department name** | Which department this is |
| **Total** | How many people work in this department |
| **Present** | Employees who checked in today |
| **Leave** | Employees on approved leave |
| **Late** | Employees who arrived after shift start |
| **Absent** | Employees with no check-in and no leave |

### Attendance percentage

Shows what percentage of your team (excluding those on pre-approved leave) actually came to work.

**How it's calculated:**
```
Attendance % = (Present + On Leave) / Total Employees × 100
```

For example:
- Department has 20 employees
- 18 checked in on time
- 1 is on approved vacation
- 1 is absent
- **Attendance = (18 + 1) / 20 = 95%**

## How to use the report

### Check today's attendance

1. Go to **Attendance Tracking**
2. Quickly scan each department card
3. Look for departments with high absent counts (red numbers)

### Click on a department for details

1. Click any department card
2. You'll see a list of employees with their individual status
3. You can see names, positions, and exact check-in/out times

### Monitor trends

1. Check the report daily or weekly
2. Watch for patterns (e.g., Monday absences, late arrivals on certain days)
3. Use this to identify problem areas or schedule issues

### Investigate issues

If a department has unusually high absences:

1. Click the department card
2. See which employees are marked absent
3. Check if they submitted leave requests that weren't approved yet
4. Contact the employees to understand the issue

## Use cases and examples

### Example 1: Morning check-in review

At 9:30 AM, HR reviews the attendance report:

- **Sales team** — 18/20 present, 1 on leave, 1 absent (late to arrive)
- **Operations** — 15/15 present, all on time
- **Support** — 12/13 present, 1 late arrival

HR spots the Sales team member who's absent and calls to check in. Turns out they forgot to submit leave and were running late.

### Example 2: Weekly attendance analysis

By Friday afternoon, HR reviews the week's attendance:

- **Operations team** has 3 employees who are frequently late
- **Support team** has high absence on Mondays

HR might:
1. Check if there are shift scheduling issues
2. Speak with team leads about patterns
3. Recommend solutions (flexible start time, shift change, etc.)

### Example 3: Leave vs. Absence

The report shows that **Engineering team** has:
- 5 employees on approved leave (blue)
- 2 employees absent (red)

This tells HR that the 5 on leave were planned (vacation approved), but the 2 absences are unplanned and need follow-up.

## Common questions

**Q: Why does the attendance percentage seem low even though employees are on approved leave?**

A: The percentage counts employees on approved leave as "present for calculation purposes." If the percentage is still low, it means employees are actually absent (without approved leave). Check the red numbers.

**Q: Can I see historical attendance data?**

A: This report shows today's data. For historical analysis, you can use the **Attendance Tracking** view and filter by date range to see past days.

**Q: What about remote employees or employees from other offices?**

A: If your company uses GPS or WiFi check-in, the system tracks them. If an employee is marked absent but says they worked remotely, you may need to manually adjust their attendance (if your system allows it).

**Q: How often does the report update?**

A: The report updates in real-time as employees check in. New check-ins appear within a few seconds.

## Tips for using this effectively

1. **Check early in the day** — Review attendance in the morning to catch any issues early
2. **Look for patterns** — Weekly or monthly reviews help you spot problems
3. **Cross-check with leave requests** — Make sure absent employees don't have pending leave approvals
4. **Share with managers** — Managers can use this to understand their team's attendance
5. **Follow up on absences** — Prompt follow-up prevents habitual absences
